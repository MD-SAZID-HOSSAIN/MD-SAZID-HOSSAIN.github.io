About

A student engineer who ships what he trains

I'm a Computer Science and Engineering student at the University of Asia Pacific, building software with a focus on machine learning and deep learning. I like working across the full stack — from Django backends to the models that power them.

Most of my projects start as a research question and end as something you can actually use: a food classifier that became a calorie estimator inside a health platform, a segmentation model tested against a second dataset to see where it breaks. I'm always picking up new tools and getting better at the ones I already use.

Smart Health Companion

Python · Django · PyTorch · REST APIs

A full-stack health-tracking platform covering daily activity logging through to AI-driven diet planning. A custom EfficientNet-B0 classifier trained on Bangladeshi and Indian dishes estimates calories from food photos. Also integrates a third-party AI API for personalized diet plans and a doctor-finder feature.

Bangladeshi Food Classifier

Python · PyTorch · EfficientNet-B0

The vision model behind Smart Health Companion's calorie feature. Fine-tuned an EfficientNet-B0 on a custom merged dataset of Indian and Bangladeshi dishes, handling visually distinct dishes well and confusing the ones that look alike.

Brain Tumor Segmentation

Python · PyTorch · U-Net · ResNet-34

A U-Net trained to segment brain tumors from FLAIR MRI slices, then evaluated on a separate Figshare dataset to measure how far it generalizes. Includes a failure analysis of the domain shift between MRI scan types.

  • val_dice0.918
  • cross_datasetFigshare

Zero-Shot Image Classification

Python · PyTorch · Hugging Face (CLIP)

A benchmark pitting OpenAI's zero-shot CLIP against a conventionally fine-tuned ResNet18. Covers prompt engineering and looks at when a model trained on general web imagery beats a supervised model on a specific domain.
