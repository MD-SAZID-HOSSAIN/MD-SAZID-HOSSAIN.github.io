Bangladeshi Food Classifier

Python, PyTorch, EfficientNet-B0

The vision model behind Smart Health Companion's food calorie feature. Fine-tuned an EfficientNet-B0 on a custom merged dataset of Indian and Bangladeshi dishes (16 classes). Reached 77.7% validation accuracy, effectively handling visually distinct dishes.

View on Kaggle

Brain Tumor Segmentation

Python, PyTorch, U-Net, ResNet-34

A U-Net model trained to segment brain tumors from MRI scans and evaluated on a cross-dataset (Figshare) to measure generalization. Reached 0.918 validation Dice on FLAIR MRI slices. Conducted detailed failure analysis on domain shifts between MRI scan types.

View on Kaggle

Zero-Shot Image Classification

Python, PyTorch, Hugging Face (CLIP)

A benchmark comparing OpenAI's zero-shot CLIP against a conventionally fine-tuned ResNet18 across three datasets. Explored prompt engineering and analyzed when a general web imagery model outperforms a supervised model on specific domains.
